MARKET CONTEXT
Rapido's Fleet Aggregator Challenge
Rapido operates one of India's largest shared mobility networks — managing thousands of vehicles through fleet aggregators. Running a profitable aggregator fleet demands intelligence at every layer, but most operators are working with near-zero data.


No Real-Time Fleet Visibility
Aggregators manage hundreds of vehicles with no live insight into location, movement status, or whether vehicles are in active service or sitting idle.


Vehicle Health is a Blind Spot
Without diagnostics, SOC, or engine health data — breakdowns are unpredictable. Every surprise failure is a service gap and a revenue loss for the aggregator.


Utilisation is Guesswork
Without KM tracking, trip counts, and active-hour data — operators cannot tell which vehicles earn and which vehicles idle. Every fleet decision is a guess.


Fleet Costs are Invisible
Fuel spend, cost per km, and per-vehicle operating expense are tracked manually if at all. Fleet profitability is impossible to calculate without this foundation.
Transight Compass answers all four — in one connected platform.

FLEET ACCOUNTING — TRANSIGHT MILES
Vehicle Utilisation Intelligence
Transight Miles defines, measures, and flags utilisation at the individual vehicle level — giving aggregators precise, real-time intelligence on every asset in their fleet.


DAILY KM PER VEHICLE
Actual KM vs Target
Each vehicle's daily distance plotted against a defined target (e.g. 80 km/day). Vehicles consistently below target are flagged — vehicles exceeding threshold trigger maintenance alert.


UTILISATION RATE
Active Hrs ÷ Available Hrs
Trip-active hours divided by total scheduled hours, expressed as a utilisation %. A vehicle available for 12 hrs but active for 4 hrs = 33% utilisation — immediately visible to the aggregator.


IDLE ENGINE TIME
Engine-On Without Movement
Engine running minutes with zero movement, as a % of total engine-on time. High idle % = fuel being burned with no revenue generated — a direct cost signal for the aggregator.


TRIP FREQUENCY INDEX
Trips Completed per Day
Number of distinct trips completed per vehicle per day. Low trip count on a vehicle that is geographically available indicates deployment or driver efficiency issue.


UNDER-UTILISATION FLAG
< Threshold for 3+ Days
Vehicle falling below defined daily KM or trip target for 3 or more consecutive days is flagged for aggregator review — redeploy, rotate, or investigate before cost accumulates.


OVER-UTILISATION ALERT
> Threshold — Maintenance Risk
Vehicles exceeding optimal km ceiling are flagged for priority maintenance scheduling. Running beyond service thresholds is a breakdown risk — caught before failure, not after.

FLEET ACCOUNTING — TRANSIGHT MILES
KM per Fleet & Fuel Cost Intelligence
Device-reported odometer and CAN fuel data — converted by Transight Miles into per-vehicle, per-fleet financial metrics that aggregators can act on daily.
KM per Fleet — Defined Metrics


FLEET DISTANCE SUMMARY
Daily / Weekly / Monthly KM
Odometer per vehicle rolled up to fleet total. Trend lines show if fleet productivity is rising or declining period-over-period.


ENGINE HOURS VS KM RATIO
Idle Burn Detector
High engine hours + low KM = excessive idling. Reveals vehicles burning fuel with zero productive output — flagged immediately.


FLEET AVERAGE VS VEHICLE
Outlier Detection
Vehicles consistently below fleet-average daily KM are surfaced automatically. Aggregators see laggards without reviewing each vehicle manually.


ROUTE-LEVEL KM ANALYSIS
Trip Distance vs Expected
Actual vs expected trip distance — flags detours, short-trips, and unauthorised deviations that inflate operating cost.
Fuel Cost per KM — Defined Metrics


FUEL CONSUMED PER 100 KM
Efficiency Benchmark (L/100km)
CAN-reported fuel ÷ distance driven — per vehicle and fleet average. Vehicles above fleet average L/100km flagged for engine health review.


FUEL COST PER KM (₹)
₹/KM — Per Vehicle & Fleet
Fuel spend ÷ km driven, per vehicle and fleet-wide. Identifies the most expensive vehicles to operate — core input for profitability decisions.


FUEL EFFICIENCY DEGRADATION
Month-on-Month Trend
Declining km/l across periods = early engine maintenance warning. Transight Miles surfaces this before the vehicle fails in the field.


IDLE FUEL BURN (₹ LOST)
Engine-On Cost Without Movement
Idle time × consumption rate = ₹ wasted per vehicle per period. Puts a rupee value on idle behaviour — the metric that changes operator decisions fastest.

FLEET ACCOUNTING — TRANSIGHT MILES
Fleet Expense Accounting & Financial Visibility
Transight Miles brings financial clarity to every vehicle — giving aggregators the per-vehicle economics they need to manage a profitable fleet, not just a tracked one.


TOTAL COST OF OPERATION (TCO)
₹ per Vehicle per Month
Fuel + maintenance + downtime cost aggregated into a single TCO figure per vehicle. The definitive number for measuring fleet profitability at vehicle level.


COST PER KM
₹/KM — Fleet vs Vehicle
Total operating cost divided by distance driven — per vehicle and fleet average. Instantly identifies the most expensive vehicles relative to the distance they cover.


REVENUE PER KM INDEX
Earnings ÷ Distance Driven
When trip revenue data is available — revenue generated per km driven surfaces the true earning efficiency of each vehicle, not just its operational cost.


MAINTENANCE COST ATTRIBUTION
₹ per 10,000 KM
Miles-driven triggers maintenance scheduling — tyre replacement cycles, oil change intervals, service milestones. Spreads maintenance cost across actual usage, not calendar time.


EXPENSE TREND — PERIOD ANALYSIS
Month-on-Month per Vehicle
Per-vehicle cost tracked over consecutive periods. Aggregators see spending trajectories and intervene before any vehicle's cost-per-km exceeds the fleet threshold.


FLEET BUDGET VS ACTUAL
Planned vs Consumed (₹)
Planned fuel and maintenance spend benchmarked against actual — per vehicle and fleet-wide. Enables precise budget control for the aggregator across any fleet size.
